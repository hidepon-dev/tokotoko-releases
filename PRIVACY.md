# Privacy Policy for TokoToko Gemini Integration

Last updated: September 30, 2026

TokoToko Gemini Integration ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Extension.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
* **No Personal Data Collected:** The Extension does not collect, log, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive personal data.
* **No Chat Content Logged:** The Extension does not read, store, or transmit the content of your prompts or AI responses.
* **Local Operation Only:** The Extension only observes the UI state of Google Gemini (such as typing, generating, or completed) locally in your browser to trigger desktop animations.
* **Local Communication:** The status signals are communicated strictly locally to your desktop TokoToko application via Native Messaging. No data is sent over the internet or to external servers.
* **Settings Storage:** Custom reaction preferences are stored locally in your browser using Chrome Storage API and synced only within your personal Google account if Chrome Sync is enabled.

## 2. Third-Party Sharing
The Extension does not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties.

## 3. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
